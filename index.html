    A note from the lab

    You can now remaster in two ways

    Audio Remastering Lab now includes Studio Pro, a second console for a more detailed remaster. The two buttons under the title let you switch whenever you want. Your song stays loaded either way.

    Classic The original simple page. A few sliders for EQ, loudness, stereo, reverb, and warmth.
    Studio Pro The new detailed console. More EQ bands, tools for harsh AI tracks, stereo imaging, fades, and loudness meters.
